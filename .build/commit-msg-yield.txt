THE PURSUIT YIELDS WHEN THE CAR IS NOT ADVANCING, AND A CHUNK SWITCH CLEARS
THE WINDOW

Two defects, both MEASURED in round chunk3 (2026-11-10), both in the pursuit's
own criteria.

1. THE CRITERION WAS "THE ERROR IS LARGE", AND A PINNED CAR HAS A SMALL ERROR.

   MEASURED (round chunk3, the last pursuit phase, every 30 frames):

       f     perr     along    pdist   total   steer  thr brk owner
       5190  -0.4892   123.85    9.47   34501   0.978   1  0  pursuit
       5340  -0.5307   123.87    9.50   34507   1.000   1  0  pursuit

   `along` is FROZEN (123.85 -> 123.87 over 150 frames) and so is the guest's
   own progress (34,501 -> 34,507), while the pursuit holds steer = -1.000
   (FULL LOCK) with the throttle on and NO recovery (brk = 0).

   The limit-cycle detector needed an error above 1.40 rad; this state has
   0.53 rad, so it never fired, and the car stayed pinned for the rest of the
   round.  **A CAR PINNED AGAINST A BARRIER WITH A 30 DEGREE ERROR IS STILL
   PINNED.**  The criterion is now the car's own PROGRESS ALONG THE TRACK:

       notAdvancing = (the car advanced less than 1.0 m in the last 120 frames)

   and the error condition is kept as an ADDITIONAL trigger, not as the only
   one.  The threshold is a DISTANCE and not a time, because a distance is what
   tells "pinned" from "crawling": the pinned car measured above advanced
   0.02 m in 3.6 s, and a car crawling along a barrier at 0.3 m/s covers
   1.08 m in the same window.

2. THE ALONG-TRACK COORDINATE IS A PROPERTY OF ITS CHUNK, AND THE WINDOW WAS
   NOT CLEARED WHEN THE CHUNK CHANGED.

   MEASURED (round chunk3): the coordinate jumped from 32.26 to 132.11 with the
   car in the same place -- the chunk picker had moved to another chunk -- and
   the flip criterion's window read that jump as **205.21 m of progress the
   car never made**.  A window that spans two chunks is not a measurement of
   anything.

   The sample now carries the chunk the coordinate was measured on
   (`targetChunk`), the race-state line reports it (`tchunk=`), and the window
   is CLEARED whenever the chunk changes: the ring, the anchor and the stuck
   timer all restart, and the criteria begin again from the new chunk.

THE NEW LOG FIELDS: `tchunk=` (the chunk the coordinate belongs to) and
`adv=` (how far the car advanced along the track in the last 120 frames).
The second one is what the yield criterion reads, so it is on the record.
