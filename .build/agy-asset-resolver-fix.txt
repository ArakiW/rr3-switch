native-switch/source/guest_filesystem.cpp

      THE FUNCTION THAT FAILS.  It takes the asset list's path (which starts
      with '/', e.g. "/assets_2048x1536/common/sprites.android.atlas") and

      entries, so the candidate list it tries is nearly right -- and it fails
      for exactly the paths whose files sit under .depot/.

  The rewrite site is around line 5889-5934 (the "asset_list_updates.txt"
  pass) and there are equivalent passes for the other lists (the log shows
  "assetlist-rewrite-all-begin lists=1235 overrides=8649").

  There is ALSO a separate OVERRIDE SET of 8,649 known paths with their
  digests.  Work out whether the 45 failures are (a) resolveAssetLocalFile
  not trying the right prefix, or (b) entries that are not in the override
  set, or (c) both -- and say which, with the code.
