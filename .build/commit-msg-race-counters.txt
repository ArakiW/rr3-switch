THE RACE MARKERS ARE COUNTED BY THE PORT, NOT BY THE LOG

The round census counted "start lights" and "race screen" by REGEXING the
guest-view log for the strings "StartLightsScreen" and "InGameScreen".  The
only occurrences of those strings left in the log are the port's OWN
path-trace lines, and their number is decided by the port's logging policy,
not by the game:

  * the trace is thinned to gui/processed/ and de-duplicated by a 2048-slot
    table with no eviction, so a path is written AT MOST ONCE for the whole
    run -- "StartLightsScreen.gui" can produce at most one map-app line no
    matter how many times the guest asks for that screen;
  * the guest's own log stream is quota-limited (kAndroidLogAnyBudget = 4000
    in guest_view_trace.cpp:37), and the archived round mapgate1 ends with
    the ordinal at EXACTLY 4000, so the guest's own screen names are not in
    the part that survived.

So the number was the trace's policy all along: 27 in round yield2 (before
the thinning) and 2 after it, from the same game.  The experiment section
9.44.3 proposed -- "the same binary with the trace restored" -- would have
restored the count without changing what was counted.

THE FIX.  The port keeps the two markers as COUNTS, incremented in
logResolvedPathOnce() BEFORE the thinning and BEFORE the dedup, i.e. as
RESOLUTION EVENTS, which is what the census always meant to measure:

  * guest_filesystem.cpp: g_startLightsOpens / g_raceScreenOpens, with
    guestStartLightsOpens() / guestRaceScreenOpens() accessors, read with
    the same relaxed atomic pattern as the existing layout counters;
  * guest_view_probe.cpp: the census line now carries lights=%llu race=%llu
    next to gui=%llu twosq=%llu;
  * tools/Test-OnEden.ps1: the round census reads the PORT's counters.  The
    old text counts survive as msStartLightsText / msRaceScreenText, as a
    cross-check: a port whose counters and whose log disagree is itself
    worth knowing about;
  * a new contract test, native-switch/tests/test_guest_race_marker_contract.py,
    parses the body of logResolvedPathOnce() and asserts the counters are
    incremented BEFORE the thinning, so the bug cannot come back.

MEASURED (round racecount1, 400 s, same host): 30 start-lights resolutions
and 45 race-screen resolutions from the port's counters, against the old text
count of 2 and 2.  Frames 2400, pixels lit 921600 of 921600, md5 mismatch 0,
TrackDesc::load failures 0.

The counters count RESOLUTION EVENTS, not completed races, so 30 is a lower
bound on the races in 400 s and 45 is a count of layout requests (the race
screen is re-resolved during a race).  docs/OPEN-ISSUES.md section 9.47 has
the whole chain, and it also corrects the two stale readings that section
9.47 settles: section 9.44.3's "next experiment" and section 9.46's closing
sentence, which called this a game regression.
