THE FRAME PACE WAS PAYING FOR THE WORK TWICE

MEASURED 2026-11-10 (round chunk3, the census lines the port already
writes).  The port has carried a frame-time instrument since the beginning:
the guest-frame-census line reports the previous iteration's four numbers
every 60 frames, so the measurement costs no extra log writes.

    tt  the whole iteration
    tr  the guest's render call (the port calls the guest's render entry)
    ts  eglSwapBuffers -- the present
    tp  the port's own per-frame work outside those two (tt - tr - ts)

Round chunk3, the RACE phase (frames > 2500, 2,841 census samples):

    tt = 43.59 ms   tr = 39.29 ms   ts = 3.94 ms   tp = 0.37 ms

So the game runs at 22.9 FPS in a race, and the round's own average (5,280
frames in 406 s = 13.0 fps) is the average of that and the loading phase
(tt = 125.8 ms there), not a third number.

AND THE PACE WAS WRONG.  The frame loop slept a FIXED 16.67 ms AFTER the
work (guest_view_probe.cpp:9988), so the frame PERIOD was the work PLUS a
whole frame: every millisecond of the guest's own work was paid for twice,
and 43.6 ms of work produced a 60.3 ms frame -- 16.6 fps -- instead of the
22.9 fps the work alone allows.

THE SLEEP IS NOW THE REMAINDER OF THE BUDGET, so the period is
max(work, 16.67 ms): the port runs at 60 Hz whenever the work fits in the
budget, and an idle frame still sleeps the whole budget, so the front end
does not spin either.

THE BUDGET IS CONVERTED EXPLICITLY: armGetSystemTick() counts at
armGetSystemTickFreq() (19.2 MHz on the console) while svcSleepThread() takes
NANOSECONDS, and mixing the two would make the budget 19.2 times too long.
The main thread's own 60 Hz wait (the one that parks while the render worker
runs) is a different loop and is left alone.

AND THE REST OF THE FRAME IS MEASURED TOO, because 60 FPS needs all of it:
90 % of the frame is inside the guest's render call, and the port writes 42
log lines per frame (scissor 7.1, map-app 4.8, cc-dispatch 6.1, jni-* 9.8),
every one of them an fprintf plus an fflush on the card.  The path-resolution
log is deduped but the RESOLUTION is not: resolveAssetLocalFile() maps the
path and touches the card on every request, and the guest asks for the same
assets over and over.

docs/OPEN-ISSUES.md gains 9.29 with the numbers, and .build/frame-stats.py
computes them the same way every time, so two rounds can be compared.
