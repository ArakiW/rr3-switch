THREE THINGS THAT WERE NOT MEASURED BEFORE ARE NOW:

1. THE CAR CAN SIT STILL WITH THE PURSUIT AT FULL LOCK AND THE THROTTLE ON,
   AND NOTHING CATCHES IT IN TIME.  `along` is frozen (123.85 -> 123.87 over
   150 frames) and so is the guest's progress (34,501 -> 34,507), while
   `steer` is at full lock, `thr` is 1 and `brk` is 0.  The limit-cycle
   detector needs an error above 1.40 rad and this state has 0.53 rad, so it
   does not fire; the stuck test needs 600 frames (18.2 s) of no progress and
   the round ended first.  => THE CRITERION FOR "THE PURSUIT MUST YIELD" HAS
   TO BE "THE CAR IS NOT ADVANCING", NOT "THE ERROR IS LARGE": a car pinned
   against a barrier with a 30 degree error is still pinned.

2. THE ALONG-TRACK COORDINATE IS NOT COMPARABLE ACROSS A CHUNK SWITCH.  The
   coordinate is measured on the chunk the picker chose, and the picker
   changed: `along` is 32.26 at f=4530 and 132.11 at f=4800 with the car in
   the same place, so the flip criterion's window saw a +100 m "progress"
   that never happened and printed `fdelta = 205.21`.  => THE RING HAS TO BE
   CLEARED WHEN THE CHUNK CHANGES, or the criterion has to be measured on a
   chunk-independent quantity.

3. THE PORT CAN NOW MEASURE WHY A CAR IS STUCK: `pdist` (the live distance to
   the line) and `along` (the arc coordinate) together say "13 m off the line
   and not moving along the track for 150 frames".  Before this round the same
   fact was invisible, because `lineResidual` held the FIT's residual, which
   is a constant.
